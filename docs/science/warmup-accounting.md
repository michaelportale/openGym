Warm-up sets are saved and shown in history, and they count toward how long the session lasted. They are left out of progression, personal records, estimated one-rep max, and volume, because a warm-up is priming rather than the work itself.

Our own inference: None. The exclusion is the definition of a warm-up in this app, not a finding from a study.
