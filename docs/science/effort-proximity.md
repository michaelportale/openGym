You can log how many reps you had left on a set. A report of many reps left is shown as far from failure. Muscle growth looks better closer to failure, and strength looks similar across a wide range. This is exploratory, and the reps-left number is an estimate of how close the set was.

Our own inference: Showing an optional per-set estimate, and wording a high number as far from failure, is our display of an abstract-level result. Many reps left means a hand-entered rating above the inherited HARD_RIR of 3. It does not change the next session by itself.
