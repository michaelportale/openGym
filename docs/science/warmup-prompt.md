A warm-up is prompted on the first lift for a muscle group in the session, not on later lifts for that muscle. That timing is our inference. The summary we read supports skipping a warm-up when the load is already near a 10RM. Evidence for the timing itself is thin.

Our own inference: Prompting only the first lift for a muscle group is our inference from the summary's support for skipping loads already near a 10RM. The summary does not itself test that session rule. The muscle group used here is the catalogue body part. A later lift for a new body part is still prompted.
