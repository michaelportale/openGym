The first two sets of a lift are 6 reps at about 40% and 80% of the weight you are about to use, rounded down to a weight you can load, and never below the bar. An 80 lb dumbbell work weight is about 30 and 60. Evidence is limited: one crossover study, and the result is for the bench press.

Our own inference: The 2 by 6 at 40% and 80% is the product decision taken from that summary. Rounding down onto a loadable weight, and never going below the bar, is our own rule. Native warm-ups use that prescription. When the work weight is at or under the bar, native returns no warm-up sets. The shared helper in frontend still closes half the gap, and this lane does not edit it.
