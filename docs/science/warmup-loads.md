The first two sets of a lift are 6 reps at about 40% and 80% of the weight you are about to use, rounded down to a weight you can load, and never below the bar. An 80 lb dumbbell work weight is about 30 and 60. Evidence is limited: one crossover study, and the result is for the bench press.

Our own inference: The 2 by 6 at 40% and 80% is the product decision taken from that summary. Rounding down onto a loadable weight, and never going below the bar, is our own rule. The shared warm-up helper still ramps by closing half the gap to the work weight, not by 40/80. This entry records the decision and does not change that helper.
